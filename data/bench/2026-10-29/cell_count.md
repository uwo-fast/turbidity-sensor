Hemocytometer count of the `c0500` bottle (half the culture as grown).

## Count

The five small 0.2 mm squares of the central square (0.004 µL each), sample diluted 1:1 in
supernatant.

| Square       |                         Cells |
| ------------ | ----------------------------: |
| Top left     | 138 (counted twice: 135, 138) |
| Top right    |                           143 |
| Middle       |                           133 |
| Bottom right |                           159 |
| Bottom left  |                           148 |
| Mean ± SD    |                  144.2 ± 10.0 |

721 cells in all, so the counting error is about 4 % (1/√721).

## Cells per mL

Cells/mL = mean per square × dilution × (1 / 0.004 µL)

= 144.2 × 2 × 2.5 × 10⁵ = 7.2 × 10⁷ cells/mL at `c0500`

The common × 10⁴ formula is for the large 1 mm squares (0.1 µL) and does not apply here.
